A full-time registered nurse in Luxembourg earns between **€5,565 and €10,859 gross per month** in 2026. Where in that range depends on two things: where they work, and for how long.

The job is the same, but it falls under two different collective agreements: the **CCT FHL** for hospitals, and the **CCT SAS** for the care and social sector (home care, nursing homes, social care organisations). Each has its own pay scale, its own working week and its own index point value.

All amounts below were calculated on 22 November 2026 with [Salaire Santé](https://salairesanteluxembourg.com/en/), the salary simulator I build from the texts of both agreements.

## How the salary is calculated

Both agreements use the same formula for the base salary:

> number of points × index point value × hours worked / reference hours

- The **number of points** depends on the career level (C5 under the SAS, CS7 under the FHL for a registered nurse) and on seniority.
- The **index point value** follows Luxembourg’s automatic wage indexation. Since the index tranche of 1&nbsp;June 2026 (application rate 992.24), it is **€23.98572** under the SAS and **€24.51349** under the FHL.
- The **reference week** is **40 hours** under the SAS and **38 hours** under the FHL.

Example: a nurse starting under the SAS has 237 points, so 237 × €23.98572 = **€5,684.62 gross per month**.

## The pay scale year by year

Base gross monthly salary, full time, by years of recognised experience:

| Seniority | Home care, nursing homes (CCT SAS, 40 h) | Hospital (CCT FHL, 38 h) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starting | €5,684.62 | €5,564.56 |
| 2 years | €5,972.44 | €5,981.29 |
| 5 years | €6,404.19 | €6,398.02 |
| 10 years | €7,171.73 | €7,648.21 |
| 15 years | €7,915.29 | €8,457.15 |
| 20 years | €8,658.84 | €9,633.80 |
| 25 years | €9,426.39 | €10,442.74 |
| 30 years | €9,882.12 | €10,859.47 |

At the start, the SAS scale is slightly higher. From 10 years on, the hospital pulls clearly ahead: after 20 years the gap is more than €970 a month.

## Mind the working week

A month-by-month comparison hides one difference: a hospital nurse works 2 hours less per week. Scaled to 40 hours, their starting salary would be €5,857, more than the €5,685 paid under the SAS. **Per hour, the hospital pays more from the first year.**

## Over the year: year-end allocation and holiday bonus

Both agreements add a year-end allocation and a holiday bonus to the monthly salary. Total annual gross pay becomes:

| Seniority | CCT SAS | CCT FHL |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starting | €74,907 | €77,693 |
| 10 years | €94,240 | €104,780 |
| 30 years | €129,475 | €146,526 |

## What these figures don’t tell you

- **These are gross amounts.** Net pay depends on your tax class and family situation.
- **Allowances are not included**: night work, Sundays, public holidays, on-call duty.
- **Seniority means recognised experience**, not only the years with your current employer. A career abroad or in another sector can be taken into account, fully or partly, under each agreement’s rules.
- **Amounts move with the index.** Each new index tranche raises the point value by 2.5%, and every salary with it.
